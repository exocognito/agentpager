**The shape of an area file:**
- A header callout: Definition, Serves (Sam's phrases quoted), Relies on, Code, Screens, Walked by.
- Then `## Behaviors`, `## Invariants`, `## Metrics` and `## Limits`, leaving out any that are empty.
- Each entry is `### <ID> · @M<n> · <short promise>`, then an optional `Why:`, then numbered clauses (promises, ID.n) and bullets (notes).
- `@M<n>:` at the start of a clause means a later milestone added it.
- A limit's bullets cover: what people meet / why it waits / what would fix it / when to revisit.
