## Calls for the build

Choices agents make, proposed here for the root to confirm and give to the builders; each lives in code once built.
- The move copies. The old stores keep what they held, unused, until a release after TIDY's removes them, after a tag (Out of scope). An agent's old store, once its rows have moved, writes nothing again and passes every call on to its pager's store.
- Moving one pager is one transaction in its new store, and moving it again finds it done, so a move cut off by a restart or a deploy runs again from the start.
- M5's routes stay, so an agent's calls can still name only its agent id. New routes may add the pager id, which the command's file already holds.
- The phone keeps its route and its reply for a pager's read.
- A closed decision travels as a field the `m5` clients ignore, with its status unchanged on the wire, so the `m5` app still decodes every read; an answer to it is refused with words that say why.
- The wait is held in memory in the pager's store and checks the pager is on before it returns an answer; at its time limit it returns pending, as now, so a lost wake costs one wait.
- The upgrade check only reads the `m5` baseline: no connect to its pager, no answer, no acknowledgement. Moved data's full loop is proven locally (PR 4).
- [A] One store per pager holds everything in SQLite tables keyed by agent and decision, as each agent's store does now; the builder chooses the schema.
