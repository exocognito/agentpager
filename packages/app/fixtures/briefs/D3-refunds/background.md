# Refund requests this month

The support bot hands every refund request to a person, whatever the amount. Here’s what that queue looks like.

## Requests are up 50% in four weeks
| Week of | Requests |
|---------|----------|
| Sep 29  | 80       |
| Oct 6   | 95       |
| Oct 13  | 110      |
| Oct 20  | 120      |

## 7 in 10 are under $50
Last week’s 120 requests, by amount.

|             |    |     |
|-------------|----|-----|
| Under $50   | 84 | 70% |
| $50 to $200 | 30 | 25% |
| Over $200   | 6  | 5%  |

## The wait is the complaint
|                                              |               |
|----------------------------------------------|---------------|
| Average wait for a person                    | 9 h           |
| Refund complaints, last month to this        | :red[14 → 29] |

Complaints doubled this month. Most are about how long the refund takes, not whether it’s approved.

## Abuse is rare
Last quarter, 2% of requests under $50 were flagged as abuse.

|         |     |
|---------|-----|
| Genuine | 98% |
| Flagged | 2%  |
