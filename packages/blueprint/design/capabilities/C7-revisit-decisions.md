statement: Reopen any answered decision to read it as it was asked, with your answer and the agent's acknowledgement, and nothing to change.
caveats:
  - History is read on the phone that made the pager; nothing proves it on another device.
  - The phone keeps an image or page it has opened only in memory. While the service can't be reached, one it has not opened since the app started can't be shown.
---

## Behaviors

1. Each project on a pager folds its answered decisions into an "N answered" row (C23), which lists them newest first. Each row shows the chosen option's letter or a note's bubble, the question, who asked and the answer, and a green tick once the agent has acknowledged; until then it says where the answer is (C5).
2. Reopening an answered decision shows the brief as it was asked, with when it was asked and answered, and the chosen option's whole column outlined in the Options table.
3. Its bar is the closed record: "Closed decisions can't be changed.", then the chosen option, such as "You chose A · Small + manual", and when, or the owner's note in its place; then the agent's confirmation and next steps beside a green tick. There is nothing to choose or send.
4. Answered decisions stay readable while the pager is off. They last until the pager is deleted, or until their agent has had no activity for 30 days (I17).
5. While the service can't be reached, the phone shows its last read of each pager.
