## Horizons

MACCOUNT and MPRORESEARCH are parked in [parked/](parked/) with their promises. MACCOUNT defines accounts and cross-device ownership, with authentication, recovery and privacy still draft. Its proposed deliverables and preview exist for review, but it is not in the ordered list. No email-only or library or provider choice is approved.

MPRORESEARCH defines the $2.99/month research offer and consented survey, not a subscription or unlimited-session entitlement. The preview says buying is not implemented. Survey delivery and retention still need design; it stays outside the ordered list.

Review what works with users at each stage. Native iOS and Android, community-contributed cards, and Pro general availability remain draft direction. Native value and distribution, contribution trust and compatibility, and real payment, support and reliability need their own designs. Research signals guide those choices; no stage promises revenue or organic growth.

Other deferred candidates: Alchemy when the owner sets its date, background notifications, recovery for a lost guest phone key, a real 24-hour deployed expiry wait, and SG1/SG2/SG3 outcome metrics once they have bars.
