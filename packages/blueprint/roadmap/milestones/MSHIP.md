# MSHIP · AgentPager for people I invite

## Objective

Someone I invite installs AgentPager from TestFlight and tries the demo agent from agentpager.me before connecting their own. Scanning an agent's QR code with the Camera opens the app straight to pairing. When their agent asks, their phone alerts them even with the app closed, and opening the alert takes them to that decision. The website says what AgentPager stores and for how long. App Review can try the whole flow from the review notes.

C20 is listed again because Universal Links lift its first caveat. Every MAPP promise must still pass.

## Out of scope

- Answering from an alert or the Lock Screen, and Live Activities.
- Android, accounts, Pro, and product cards.
- A public App Store release before the owner decides on one.
