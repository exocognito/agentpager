# MSITE · A front page for AgentPager as it is now

## Objective

Someone who lands on agentpager.me understands in a few seconds what AgentPager does for them, sees it on the phone, and knows how to get the app and the command. The page is simple, true to the product as it is now, and looks good on a phone and a laptop. It says what the service stores and for how long. Nothing on it describes the first version.

## Out of scope

- Pricing or plans: AgentPager has no paid tier (SP2).
- A public demo: the demo agent is for App Review and invited owners (C19).

## Sam decides first

Sam workshops the page before anything is built, each round as real pages to look at: who it's for and what it must say, then its structure, then how it looks. C18's behaviours are rewritten from what the workshop settles.
