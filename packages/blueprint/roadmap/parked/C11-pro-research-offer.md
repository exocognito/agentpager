## Behaviors

Open the Pro page. It says Early research preview, shows the proposed $2.99/month price and unlimited manually ended sessions, and explains that free sessions are private, end 24 hours after pairing and retain history only within the active session. Buy shows thanks, not-implemented wording and no-payment/no-session-change wording, followed by the survey. No checkout, subscription, charge or entitlement mutation occurs: an active free session's expiry and the account's values are the same before and after Buy, and the page sends no payment request. Appearance and copy are imported from [ProScreen](design/ProScreen.tsx#ProScreen).
