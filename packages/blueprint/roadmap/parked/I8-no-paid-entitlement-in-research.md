---
id: I8::no-paid-entitlement-in-research
serves:
  - SI9
statement: Buying a Pro research stub never charges, changes an account plan or extends a session; feedback sends only after explicit consent and confirmation.
caveats:
  - Launching real unlimited sessions or subscriptions needs a later owner-approved design and proof.
---

The offer tests interest and useful work. A submission leaves plan, billing and session values unchanged. Until the real offering exists, existing free boundaries hold. No payment provider, subscription integration, checkout or billing credential belongs to this research contract. The survey payload is the [ResearchFeedback](design/research.ts#ResearchFeedback) schema, not an automatic behavioral tracking stream.
