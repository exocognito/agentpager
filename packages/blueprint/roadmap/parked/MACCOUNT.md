# MACCOUNT · Accounts and cross-device ownership

## Objective

Accounts let me return to explicitly attached active sessions from another device. The guest experience still works without sign-in, and free content remains ephemeral. Authentication methods, verifier, recovery, revocation and deletion need their own sharpened design before this draft joins the active roadmap. The preview does not commit us to email-only auth or any library/provider.

## Out of scope

- Pro entitlement, billing and changing the free-session lifetime.

This packet is draft direction, outside the active roadmap; its named dependency is not yet supplied.
