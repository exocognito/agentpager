# MPRORESEARCH · Pro research offer and user signal

## Objective

I can read a clear $2.99/month early research offer for unlimited, manually ended agent sessions. Buy thanks me but says it is not implemented, takes no payment and changes nothing. A consented survey asks what work I would do, how long I need and whether I would pay, with optional contact details so the cofounder can help. The free product still uses private sessions capped at 24 hours after pairing, with active-session history that clears at ending/expiry and no archive. The feedback collector, access and retention rules must be chosen before this draft joins the active roadmap.

## Out of scope

- Subscription integrations, payment collection and actually enabling unlimited sessions.

This packet is draft direction, outside the active roadmap; its named dependency is not yet supplied.
